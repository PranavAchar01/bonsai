Main page layouts

Five ways to arrange the page around the tree. The trees are the live demo; the text is placeholder. Every option keeps the same controls (benchmark, generations, problems, Grow, Stop) and the same data (agents, lineage, selected version). Pick one, or mix parts.

A · The scroll

The tree is the page, as the design brief says. One quiet column of margin notes on the right: the number, then the generations. Controls collapse into one sentence along the bottom. Agents run as a single ticker line. Diff and harness open in an overlay when you click a version.
