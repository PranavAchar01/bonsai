# Insurance policy interpretation

Read the policy and the claim. A: the claim is covered by the policy. B: the claim is not covered. C: it is ambiguous whether the claim is covered.

> Policy: Beth has home insurance that covers losses from "Malicious Acts or Vandalism," defined as "costs associated with repairing deliberate damage to the policyholder's property made by an adverse third party."
>
> Claim: Beth lives in a very quiet neighborhood at the edge of town with her two young children. Upon returning home from work one day,  she sees that an exterior wall of her house is covered with drawings made in permanent marker. She reviews her home surveillance footage and sees that it was her children who drew on the wall. She hires professional painters to paint over the drawing. Beth files a claim with her insurance company for the damages.

Write only the letter A, B or C to answer.txt.
