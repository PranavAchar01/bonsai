# Insurance policy interpretation

Read the policy and the claim. A: the claim is covered by the policy. B: the claim is not covered. C: it is ambiguous whether the claim is covered.

> Policy: Tom's home insurance policy includes coverage for "Wind Damage," defined as "damage from wind speeds with gusts of at least 55 mph."
>
> Claim: Tom's house is located near a large lake. One day, strong winds in excess of 55mph blow across the lake while Tom is working on his roof, but it's a loud and surprising ring from his cell phone that causes him to drop his heavy toolbox and thereby damage the roof. Tom files a claim with his insurance company for the damage.

Write only the letter A, B or C to answer.txt.
