# Insurance policy interpretation

Read the policy and the claim. A: the claim is covered by the policy. B: the claim is not covered. C: it is ambiguous whether the claim is covered.

> Policy: Cam's home insurance policy includes coverage for "Storm Damage," defined as "damage caused by a storm."
>
> Claim: One day, a lightning storm passes through Cam's neighborhood and lightning hits his neighborhood's power plant, causing a power outage. Cam goes to his attic with a candle to reset the circuit breaker, but he drops the candle and starts a fire so large that almost all of his roof burns before firefighters manage to extinguish it. Cam files a claim with his insurance company for the damage.

Write only the letter A, B or C to answer.txt.
