# Insurance policy interpretation

Read the policy and the claim. A: the claim is covered by the policy. B: the claim is not covered. C: it is ambiguous whether the claim is covered.

> Policy: Peter has insurance that covers "Loss or Damage to a Goods Carrying Vehicle," which includes "key replacement in the case of theft."
>
> Claim: As part of his bakery business, Peter owns a van which he uses to make local deliveries. One day, Peter is mugged by an individual who takes the key to the van. Replacing the key costs Peter hundreds of dollars. Peter files a claim with his insurance company for his losses.

Write only the letter A, B or C to answer.txt.
