# Insurance policy interpretation

Read the policy and the claim. A: the claim is covered by the policy. B: the claim is not covered. C: it is ambiguous whether the claim is covered.

> Policy: Genie has home insurance that covers "Garden Plants" damage, defined as "damage to plants, bushes, shrubs and trees within the boundaries of the land belonging to the home caused by civil commotion, strikes, or labor and political disturbances."
>
> Claim: Genie buys special fertilizer for her garden plants from a hardware store located next door. When the workers of the store go on strike, the store temporarily shuts down due to staff shortages, and Genie is unable to find the fertilizer within driving distance from her home or online. Without the fertilizer, the plants die. Genie files a claim with her insurance company for the damage to her plants.

Write only the letter A, B or C to answer.txt.
