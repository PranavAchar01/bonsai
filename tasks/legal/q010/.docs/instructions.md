> Policy: Hunter's car insurance policy includes coverage for "Vehicle Theft," defined as loss or damage as a result of theft to the policy holder's car or car accessories (while in or on the car)."
>

However, surveillance footage captures a masked assailant breaking into his car and stealing several hundred dollars in bills that Hunter left in the car. Hunter files a claim with his insurance company for the loss.
